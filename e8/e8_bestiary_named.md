# The E8 Bestiary: Archetypal Names for the 112 Carter Classes

## Philosophy

Each of the 112 conjugacy classes in W(E8) represents a unique symmetry transformation. We name them like creatures in a bestiary - capturing their essence through metaphor.

## The Naming System

**Primary names** based on:
- Animals/creatures for exceptional classes
- Architectural/geometric terms for classical dominance
- Mythological figures for special combinations
- Natural phenomena for

 complex mixtures

**Properties we're capturing:**
- Visual geometry (fold symmetry, vertex count)
- Numerical signature (primes in the order)
- Algebraic structure (subsystem decomposition)
- Dynamic feel (how the transformation acts)

## THE COMPLETE BESTIARY

### I. THE FIVE EXCEPTIONAL PRIMES (Pure Exceptional)

**1. E8** - **THE OUROBOROS** / **The World Serpent**
- 240 roots, 30-fold symmetry, the totality eating its tail
- Contains all others, the alpha and omega
- Order divides by 2,3,5,7
- *"In my end is my beginning"*

**2. E7** - **THE SACRED COW** / **The Hepta-Mother**
- 56 weights (7×8), 18-fold symmetry
- The 28 bitangents doubled, Aurobindo's seven levels
- Order divides by 7
- *"Seven sacred syllables, twice spoken"*

**3. E6** - **THE TRINITY** / **The Cubic Oracle**
- 27 lines on the cubic surface (3³), 12-fold
- The trinity of trinities, divine proportion
- No factor of 7 (prelapsarian)
- *"Three times three times three"*

**4. F4** - **THE TESSERACT GUARDIAN** / **The 24-Cell Keeper**
- 48 roots, 12-fold, the hypercube's heart
- Guards the threshold between classical and exceptional
- *"Four dimensions, perfectly folded"*

**5. G2** - **THE HEXAGON** / **The Primal Inchworm**
- 12 roots, 6-fold, the smallest exceptional
- Hexagonal lattice, honeycomb, the beginning
- *"Six petals of the first flower"*
